# Pitch and outreach: ForecastIQ

**Who to message:** owners and finance heads of retail chains, distributors, FMCG companies, e-commerce stores, restaurants, SaaS/subscription businesses. This product talks to decision-makers, not operations staff.

**Strongest hook:** a free forecast built from their own Excel file, including the accuracy test on their past months.

## First message

**A. English**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Name], I build dashboards that predict next month's sales from your past data, so you can plan stock and budget with more confidence. If you share 12 months of sales in Excel, I'll send you a free sample forecast.

**B. Finance angle**
> Hi [Name], budgeting next quarter on last year's numbers plus a guess? I can turn your sales history into a forecast with a realistic range, and first show you how accurate it would have been on your last 6 months. Free for one Excel file.

**C. Roman Urdu**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Name], maine aik dashboard banaya hai jo aap ke pichhle sales data se agle mahine ki sales ka andaza deta hai, range ke saath, taake stock aur budget behtar plan ho. Agar aap 12 mahine ki sales Excel mein bhej dein to main free sample forecast bana kar dikhata hoon.

Attach: `sample_monthly_report.pdf` or the 75-second video.

## Follow-ups
- **3-4 days:** "Here's what the monthly report looks like: [PDF]. Happy to make one for your business."
- **A week:** "Last note: Ramadan stock planning starts soon. Send 12 months of sales and I'll show you which products to stock up on."

## Objections

| They say | You answer |
|---|---|
| "Forecasts are never right." | That's why it shows a range, not one number, and proves itself on your own past months before you trust it. |
| "We have little data." | Start with monthly totals; the forecast improves as data grows. Below 12 months the app tells you honestly. |
| "Is our data safe?" | Login-protected, your own installation, no outside AI services, deleted on request. |
| "Our sales depend on promotions." | Promotions are in the calendar; the model learns their effect and you can test a discount in Scenarios. |
| "We already use Excel." | Keep it: upload the same export each month. Everything downloads back to Excel. |
