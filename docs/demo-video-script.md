# Demo script (5 minutes live, or 2-3 minutes as video)

`docs/demo.mp4` follows this script with on-screen captions (re-record with `python -m scripts.record_demo` once the app is ready).

| # | Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20s | Login | "How do you decide how much stock to buy for next month?" |
| 2 | 40s | Overview | Last month, next month's forecast, the range, accuracy; "solid is actual, dotted is forecast, the band is the likely range"; drivers. |
| 3 | 40s | Forecast explorer | Premium Dates: the Ramadan spike; switch branch to Karachi. |
| 4 | 30s | Overview → Attention | Body Lotion may run short at Karachi; Karachi trending down; sunscreen overstock. |
| 5 | 40s | Scenarios | Move discount to 15%: second line appears; revenue up, margin down. |
| 6 | 40s | Accuracy | "Here's how well we would have predicted your past months." |
| 7 | 20s | Reports | Monthly PDF and the short email. |
| 8 | 30s | Close | "Send me 12 months of your sales in Excel and I'll show you a forecast for your own business." |

## Before a live demo
- [ ] App started and ready (Overview loads instantly)
- [ ] If possible, a quick forecast from the prospect's own Excel (upload on the Data page, takes about a minute)
- [ ] `sample_monthly_report.pdf` ready to send after the call
- [ ] Mention the honest note: demo data is sample data; their own accuracy test is what counts
