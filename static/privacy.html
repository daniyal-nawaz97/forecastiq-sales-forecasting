Privacy & how forecasts work

Your sales data

  • Your sales file is stored only in your own installation, protected by login. It is never shared or sold.
  • All calculations run on our server with standard statistics. Your data is not sent to any outside AI service.
  • Each client has a separate installation and database.
  • Uploaded files and all forecasts are deleted on request; we confirm in writing.

What the forecast is, and isn't

  • Forecasts are estimates with a likely range (about 8 in 10 outcomes fall inside it), not promises.
  • Accuracy is tested on your own past months: each month is forecast using only the data before it.
  • More history and cleaner data give better results; 12-24 months is ideal.
  • Big one-off events (a new branch, a price change, stock shortages) should be marked in the events calendar so they don't confuse the forecast.
  • Unexpected events can't be predicted. The forecast supports decisions; it doesn't replace business judgement.

Security

  • HTTPS in production; passwords stored as salted hashes; sessions expire after 14 days.
  • Roles: admins, analysts and viewers see only what their role allows.
